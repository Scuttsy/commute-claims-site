Privacy policy

Commute Claims (Android app) · Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what the Commute Claims Android app does with your data. In short: your data stays on your phone and in your own Google account. We do not run a server and we do not receive, store or share any of your information.

Google account access

When you connect your Google account, the app asks for two permissions:

  • Read your Gmail messages (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). The app only searches for e-mails sent by NMBS/SNCB (for example no-reply@sales.belgiantrain.be) and reads their content and PDF attachments to find train tickets: stations, travel date, price and ticket number. Other e-mails are not read.
  • Send e-mail on your behalf (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send). The app only sends an e-mail when you tap “Send”: the weekly expense claim, addressed to the recipient(s) you entered in the settings, with the claim PDF and the NMBS receipts attached.

What is stored, and where

  • Ticket details and copies of the NMBS receipts are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • The details you enter in Settings (name, address, bank account/IBAN, recipient e-mail addresses, route, start date) are stored only on your phone and are used only to fill in the expense claim.
  • Sent claims are stored in your own Gmail “Sent” folder, like any e-mail you send.
  • Nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party. The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

Sharing

Your data is only shared when you explicitly send a claim, and then only with the recipients you chose. We do not sell, rent or transfer your data to anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Commute Claims' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the features described above, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Removing your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored on your phone.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the issue tracker of this site's repository.
