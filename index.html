Commute Claims

A small Android app for claiming train commuting costs.

Commute Claims finds the NMBS/SNCB train ticket confirmations in the user's own Gmail account, groups the trips for a configured route by week, and lets the user remove trips that were not work-related. With one tap it e-mails a weekly expense claim (summary PDF plus the original NMBS receipts) to the user's employer, sent from the user's own Gmail account.

How it uses Google data

  • Read Gmail (gmail.readonly) — only to search for and read e-mails sent by NMBS/SNCB.
  • Send Gmail (gmail.send) — only to send the weekly claim when the user taps “Send”.

Everything runs on the user's phone. There is no server, no account with us, no analytics and no advertising. See the privacy policy for details.
